Pricing & orders

Plan your enquiry.

We’ll confirm the details for your requested items directly.

How do I get product pricing?

Prices can vary by product, size, quantity and availability. Submit a quote request with the items and quantities you need, and we’ll follow up with a quotation.

Can I request a quote for a bulk or institutional order?

Yes. Include your product list, quantities and any specifications in the quote form so the team can prepare a relevant response.

How do I know if an item is currently available?

Product availability may change. Contact us by quote form, email, phone or WhatsApp to confirm stock and specifications before placing an order.

Do you deliver, and what are the shipping charges?

Please share your delivery location and order details when requesting a quote. Our team will confirm available delivery arrangements, timing and any applicable charges with your quotation.

What happens after I submit a quote request?

Your request is sent to our team by email. We’ll review the products and quantities you provided and contact you to discuss pricing, availability and next steps.

Can I shop or ask a quick question on WhatsApp?

Yes. Use the WhatsApp button to message 0700 471 471. Share the items you’re interested in and our team can assist with the enquiry.
